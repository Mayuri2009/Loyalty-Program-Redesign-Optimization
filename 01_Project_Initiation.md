# Loyalty Program Redesign & Optimization

## 1. Business Problem

The retailer's existing loyalty program has a high number
of enrolled customers, but customer engagement and reward
redemption are low. The business is also unable to clearly
demonstrate that the loyalty program is generating sufficient
incremental revenue or improving customer retention.

## 2. Objectives

1. Increase loyalty-member engagement and repeat purchases.
2. Provide rewards that customers actually value.
3. Simplify the reward redemption process.

## 3. Scope

### In Scope
- Current loyalty process
- Customer engagement
- Reward preferences
- Points earning and redemption
- Purchase behavior
- Competitor benchmarking
- Requirements
- Future-state process
- KPIs

### Out of Scope
- Real application development
- Production system implementation
- Actual POS integration
- Real-world customer survey
- Production financial implementation

## 4. Stakeholders

- Customers
- Marketing
- Finance
- IT
- Store Operations
- Management

## 5. Key Business Questions

- Why is loyalty engagement low?
- Which rewards do customers value?
- What prevents reward redemption?
- Does loyalty membership increase repeat purchases?
- Is the current program generating sufficient business value?

## 6. Assumptions

- Customers can enroll in the loyalty program.
- Customers earn points through eligible purchases.
- Customers can redeem points for rewards.
- Customer, transaction and loyalty data are available.

## 7. Expected Outcome

A redesigned loyalty program with clearly defined customer
requirements, improved processes, measurable KPIs and a
business case for implementation.
