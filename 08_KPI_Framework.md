# KPI Framework — Loyalty Program

## 1. Objective

The KPI framework defines the key measures used to evaluate
loyalty program engagement, customer behavior and business value.

## 2. Core KPIs

| KPI | Calculation | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Active Member Rate | Active Members / Total Members × 100 | Measures engagement |
| Redemption Rate | Members Redeeming / Total Members × 100 | Measures reward usage |
| Repeat Purchase Rate | Repeat Purchasers / Total Members × 100 | Measures repeat behavior |
| Average Member Spend | Member Revenue / Members | Measures customer value |
| Loyalty Revenue | Sum of eligible member transactions | Measures revenue contribution |

## 3. Current Baseline

| KPI | Current Value |
|---|---:|
| Active Member Rate | 36% |
| Redemption Rate | 21% |
| Repeat Purchase Rate | 42% |
| Average Member Spend | $420 |
| Loyalty Revenue | $4.2M |

## 4. KPI-to-Objective Mapping

| Business Objective | KPI |
|---|---|
| Increase loyalty engagement | Active Member Rate |
| Increase reward usage | Redemption Rate |
| Increase repeat purchases | Repeat Purchase Rate |
| Improve customer value | Average Member Spend |
| Measure business contribution | Loyalty Revenue |

## 5. Dashboard

The project will use a single-page executive Power BI dashboard
containing five KPI cards and three supporting visuals.

## 6. Key Insights

- Active member participation is 36%.
- Reward redemption is 21%.
- Active members have a higher average spend than inactive members.
- Customer research is required to understand the causes of low engagement.
