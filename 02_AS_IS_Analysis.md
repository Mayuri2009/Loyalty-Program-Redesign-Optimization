## Root-Cause Analysis: Findings and Hypotheses

### 1. Observed Business Findings

Based on the simulated portfolio dataset:

- Total enrolled customers: 10,000
- Active customers: 3,600 (36%)
- Inactive customers: 6,400 (64%)
- Customers who redeemed rewards: 2,100 (21%)
- Repeat purchasers: 4,200 (42% of the stated customer population, subject to denominator validation)

These figures indicate opportunities to investigate customer engagement, reward usage, and repeat purchasing behavior.

### 2. Potential Root-Cause Hypotheses

The following are hypotheses to investigate, not confirmed root causes:

| Hypothesis | Possible Business Impact | Evidence Needed |
|---|---|---|
| Rewards may not be sufficiently relevant to customer preferences. | Customers may have less motivation to redeem rewards. | Customer surveys, reward preference data, and redemption patterns. |
| Redemption rules or steps may be difficult to understand. | Customers may abandon or avoid redemption. | Redemption funnel data, usability testing, and customer feedback. |
| Customers may not receive relevant or timely program communications. | Customers may be less aware of available rewards. | Campaign delivery, open, click, and conversion data. |
| Some customers may have limited opportunities to earn or use points. | Members may have less incentive to participate. | Points earning history, eligibility rules, and reward availability. |

### 3. Validation Required

The available dataset can help identify customer activity and redemption patterns, but it does not independently establish why customers are inactive or why they do not redeem rewards.

The hypotheses should be validated through:

- Customer surveys or interviews.
- Review of reward eligibility and redemption rules.
- Analysis of points earning and redemption history.
- Campaign engagement analysis.
- Usability testing of the proposed redemption process.

### 4. Business Analyst's Conclusion

The initial analysis identifies low member activity and limited reward redemption as areas for further investigation. The potential causes should remain hypotheses until supported by additional evidence.

The next step is to validate these hypotheses and prioritize improvements based on customer needs, business impact, feasibility, and risk.

**Portfolio note:** This analysis is based on a simulated dataset. No real customer interviews or production investigations are claimed.
