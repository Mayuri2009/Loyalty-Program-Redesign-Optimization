# AS-IS Analysis — Existing Loyalty Program

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of the AS-IS analysis is to understand the
current customer loyalty process and identify areas where
customers experience friction or where the business may
be losing value.

## 2. Current Process

Customer
   ↓
Enroll in Loyalty Program
   ↓
Make Purchase
   ↓
Earn Points
   ↓
Points Accumulate
   ↓
Check Points Balance
   ↓
Decide Whether to Redeem
   ↓
Redeem Reward
   ↓
Use Reward

## 3. Current Pain Points

| Area | Pain Point | Business Impact |
|------|------------|-----------------|
| Enrollment | Customers may enroll but remain inactive | Low engagement |
| Rewards | Rewards may not match preferences | Low perceived value |
| Points | Customers may not monitor balances | Unused points |
| Redemption | Process may involve multiple steps | Lower redemption |
| Communication | Offers may be generic | Lower engagement |
| Measurement | Limited visibility into program value | Difficult ROI measurement |

## 4. Root Cause Analysis

### Problem
Low active usage of the loyalty program.

### 5 Whys

Why 1:
Customers are not regularly redeeming rewards.

Why 2:
Some rewards may not be attractive or relevant.

Why 3:
The program may provide limited personalization and reward choice.

Why 4:
Customer preferences and purchase behavior may not be fully
used to design offers.

Why 5:
The existing program may not have been designed around
customer segmentation and measurable engagement.

## 5. Preliminary Root Cause

The existing loyalty program may not sufficiently align
rewards and customer experience with individual customer
needs, while the business lacks strong measurement of
engagement and incremental value.
