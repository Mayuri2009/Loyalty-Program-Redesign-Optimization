# Final Business Recommendation
## Loyalty Program Redesign & Optimization

## 1. Executive Summary

The retailer's existing loyalty program has a large enrolled
customer base, but engagement and reward redemption are low.

The simulated analysis identified 10,000 loyalty members,
with 36% active members, 21% reward redemption and 42%
repeat purchasers.

Active members have an average spend of $560 compared with
$340 among inactive members.

These findings indicate an opportunity to improve loyalty
engagement and repeat purchasing.

## 2. Key Findings

### Finding 1 — Low Engagement

Only 36% of members are active.

### Finding 2 — Low Reward Usage

Only 21% of members redeemed rewards.

### Finding 3 — Spending Difference

Active members have higher average spend than inactive members.

This is an observed relationship and does not establish
causation.

## 3. Proposed Solution

The redesigned loyalty program should focus on:

- Simpler reward earning and redemption
- Clear points balance visibility
- More relevant customer offers
- Easier reward redemption
- KPI-based performance monitoring

## 4. Expected Benefits

- Increased loyalty engagement
- Increased reward redemption
- Improved customer experience
- More repeat-purchase opportunities
- Better measurement of business value

## 5. Success Metrics

- Active Member Rate
- Redemption Rate
- Repeat Purchase Rate
- Average Member Spend
- Loyalty Revenue
- Program ROI

## 6. Implementation Approach

1. Requirements & Customer Research
2. Design & Development
3. Testing / UAT
4. Launch
5. KPI Monitoring
6. Continuous Improvement

## 7. Final Recommendation

Redesign the loyalty program around simpler reward redemption,
clearer points visibility, more relevant customer offers and
measurable engagement KPIs.

Before full implementation, validate customer preferences
through customer research and test the redesigned process
through UAT.

## 8. Project Conclusion

The project demonstrates the BA lifecycle from identifying
the business problem through AS-IS analysis, requirements
gathering, data validation, TO-BE design, user stories,
UAT and KPI definition.
