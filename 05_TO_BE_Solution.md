# TO-BE Solution — Loyalty Program Redesign

## 1. Objective

The TO-BE solution defines the proposed future-state loyalty
process designed to improve customer engagement, reward
redemption and repeat purchases.

## 2. TO-BE Process

Customer
   ↓
Join Loyalty Program
   ↓
Make Eligible Purchase
   ↓
Points Automatically Added
   ↓
Customer Sees Updated Balance
   ↓
Personalized / Relevant Rewards Displayed
   ↓
Customer Selects Reward
   ↓
Simple Redemption
   ↓
Reward Automatically Applied at Checkout
   ↓
Purchase Completed
   ↓
Customer Receives Next Relevant Offer

## 3. Proposed Improvements

| Current Issue | Proposed Improvement |
|---|---|
| Customers may not know their points balance | Display current points balance |
| Rewards may not be relevant | Introduce personalized/relevant offers |
| Redemption may be complicated | Simplify redemption |
| Customers may forget rewards | Show available rewards clearly |
| Limited performance measurement | Introduce loyalty KPIs |
| Low repeat purchases | Use targeted repeat-purchase offers |

## 4. Requirement Mapping

| Requirement | TO-BE Solution |
|---|---|
| BR-001: Increase engagement | Clear points balance + relevant offers |
| BR-002: Increase repeat purchases | Targeted repeat-purchase offers |
| BR-003: Relevant rewards | Personalized reward options |
| BR-004: Simplify redemption | Simple reward selection and checkout application |
| BR-005: Measure performance | Loyalty KPI dashboard |

## 5. Expected Business Benefits

- Higher loyalty engagement
- Increased reward redemption
- Better customer experience
- Increased repeat purchase opportunities
- Better visibility into loyalty program performance

## 6. Scope Control

The solution does not include full application development,
AI recommendations, blockchain, gamification, or real POS
system integration.
